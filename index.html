Master Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Version 2.0

This Privacy Policy outlines the operational principles and compliance standards regarding personal, financial, and client data managed by Mizan Code Lab ("we", "our", or "developer") across all mobile applications distributed through the Google Play Store:

Teacher Finance Kasa Portföy Tekstil Takip Pro

1. Privacy by Architecture (Zero-Knowledge Principle)

Our applications are built on an offline-first, local-architecture framework. We do not maintain proprietary cloud databases that aggregate, store, or profile your personal data:

  • Local Storage: All student records, private lesson fees, textile production ledger entries, customer balances, asset portfolios, and financial logs reside entirely within encrypted on-device SQLite (Room) databases on your local hardware.
  • No External Harvesting: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest user telemetry, ledger balances, or identity information.

2. App-Specific Functional Scopes

  • Teacher Finance: Handles student lesson schedules, rate plans, payment reconciliation, and multi-currency balances locally. Live exchange rates and cryptocurrency telemetry are queried via read-only public endpoints without attaching personal identifiers.
  • Kasa Portföy: Tracks user-defined financial assets, transactions, and installment schedules. All investment computations remain strictly sandboxed on the host device.
  • Tekstil Takip Pro: Operates manufacturing work-orders, model costings, and wholesale customer balance tracking strictly locally. Generated statements and PDF summaries are created dynamically on-device.

3. System Permissions and Usage Justification

We request only the minimum operating system permissions required for direct user-initiated tasks:

  • Storage & Documents: Required solely when you deliberately generate, export, or import database backups, Excel/PDF reports, or invoices.
  • Network (Internet) Access: Utilized strictly for fetching live market rates (currencies/commodities) and verifying software licensing tokens.

4. Billing and In-App Transactions

For applications offering digital licenses or subscriptions, payment processing is executed exclusively via Google Play In-App Billing. Mizan Code Lab does not collect, process, or store credit card credentials, bank account credentials, or direct payment tokens.

Data Retention & Deletion Rights: Because all operational data remains on your physical device, you retain total custody. Removing the application or clearing its cache/storage via Android Settings permanently erases all databases and ledger history.
